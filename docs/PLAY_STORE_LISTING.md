### Full description

```
Smart Facts is a calm, ad-free way to learn something new every day — right from your Android home screen.

Add the Smart Facts widget and see a short teaser that updates throughout the day. Tap to read the full fact in a clean, distraction-free reader. Open the app anytime for today’s fact and a swipeable timeline of recent days.

WHAT YOU GET
• Home screen widget (Jetpack Glance) — knowledge at a glance
• Three facts per day — fresh teasers at morning, midday, and afternoon
• Polish and English — follows your device language
• Works offline — facts are cached on your device
• Many topics — science, history, nature, culture, and more
• Optional Wikipedia links when you want to read more
• No ads, no account, no tracking SDKs

HOW IT WORKS
Facts are curated in an open-source project and delivered as a small catalog your app syncs over HTTPS. New content can arrive without reinstalling the app.

PERFECT FOR
Curious minds who want a quiet daily habit — students, lifelong learners, and anyone who prefers substance over noise.

Add the widget: long-press your home screen → Widgets → Smart Facts.

Questions or feedback? Use the GitHub link on the store listing or contact the developer email shown below.
```
