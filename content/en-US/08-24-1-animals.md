A newborn giant panda weighs just **90–130 grams** (about 3–4 ounces), making it one of the smallest mammalian newborns relative to its mother's size — roughly **1/900th** of the adult female's body weight. By comparison, a human newborn is about 1/20th of its mother's weight.

The tiny size is thought to result from the panda's low-energy **bamboo diet**, which cannot support the metabolic demands of growing a larger fetus. The cubs are born blind, hairless, and nearly helpless, requiring constant warmth from the mother for the first few weeks.
