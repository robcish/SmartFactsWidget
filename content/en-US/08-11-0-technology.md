title: Backpropagation existed for decades before AI noticed it
teaser: The algorithm that powers modern deep learning was reinvented multiple times in control theory and statistics before AI researchers adopted it in the 1980s.
published: true
version: 1

Backpropagation — the method of computing gradients through a neural network to update its weights — was first derived in **1960** by Henry J. Kelley for optimal control problems. Seppo Linnainmaa formalized automatic differentiation in 1970, and Paul Werbos applied it to neural networks in his 1974 PhD thesis.

Yet the AI community remained unaware until **1986**, when David Rumelhart, Geoffrey Hinton, and Ronald Williams published their famous paper demonstrating backprop's power for learning internal representations. That decade-long gap between invention and adoption cost neural network research years of progress — the technique might have prevented the first "AI winter" entirely.
