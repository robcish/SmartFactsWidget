title: The Dunning-Kruger effect works both ways

The **Dunning-Kruger effect** (1999) shows that people with **low competence** in a skill tend to greatly **overestimate** their ability, while **experts** tend to **underestimate** theirs. This happens because beginners lack the metacognitive skills to recognize their own mistakes.
