Null Island is not a real island but the **imaginary point where the equator meets the prime meridian** in the Gulf of Guinea. Cartographers and programmers joke about it because broken geodata often defaults to 0,0.

That makes it a small lesson in digital geography. Even maps reveal where software assumptions leak into the physical world.
