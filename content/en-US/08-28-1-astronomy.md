title: A giant ring system hid a star for weeks

Astronomers think the object known as **J1407b** passed in front of its star with an enormous ring system in 2007. The eclipse pattern was so deep and so complex that it looked nothing like a normal planet transit.

The proposed rings would span tens of millions of kilometers, far larger than Saturn's, and may have contained gaps carved by forming moons. Researchers did not realize what they were looking at for years, which made the event even more startling in hindsight.
