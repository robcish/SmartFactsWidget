title: Wombats make cube-shaped droppings
teaser: Their intestines squeeze waste so unevenly that the pellets come out with flat sides.

A wombat's gut dries and compresses waste in a way that produces surprisingly regular cubes. The shape is useful because the droppings are often left on rocks or logs as scent marks, and cubes are less likely to roll away.

It is one of those strange animal facts that sounds invented until anatomy explains it.
