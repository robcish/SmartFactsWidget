**Occam's razor** advises us not to multiply assumptions beyond necessity. When two explanations fit the evidence equally well, the simpler one is often the better place to start.

That does not make simplicity a law of nature. A neat explanation can still be wrong, which is why the razor is a guide for reasoning rather than a substitute for evidence.
