In **Anarchy, State, and Utopia**, Robert Nozick imagines a tank that can feed you any experience you choose, indistinguishable from the real thing. Most readers hesitate to plug in forever, even if the tank promises happiness on tap.

Nozick used that hesitation against a simple pleasure calculus. We seem to want to **do** things, to be a certain kind of person, and to stay in contact with a world that is not only staged inside the skull.
