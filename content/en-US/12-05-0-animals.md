**Reindeer** (caribou) are the only living deer species in which females as well as males regularly grow antlers. Males usually shed theirs after the autumn rut, often by December. Females keep theirs through winter, which helps them defend feeding craters in the snow.

So a December picture of antlered reindeer pulling a sleigh is pointing, biologically, at **females** or at castrated males, not at a typical breeding bull. The racks are tools for a dark-season food fight.
