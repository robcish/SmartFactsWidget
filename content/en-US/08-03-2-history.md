Contrary to popular belief, the **Great Wall of China** is **not visible from low Earth orbit** with the naked eye. Multiple astronauts, including Yang Liwei (China's first astronaut), have confirmed this. The wall is narrow (about 5-8 meters wide) and blends with the surrounding landscape.
