Take a four-digit number whose digits are not all the same. Arrange them into the largest and smallest figures, subtract, and repeat. Indian mathematician **D. R. Kaprekar** noticed that the process reaches **6174**, then loops.

Numbers with repeated digits may hit 0 instead. For the rest, 6174 is a surprisingly small attractor: a playground result that still feels like a trick the first time a calculator obeys it.
