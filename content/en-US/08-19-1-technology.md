A **512-bit RSA key** can now be factored on a single desktop computer in roughly **73 days**. When the same key length was first broken in 1999 (RSA-155), it required a coordinated effort across hundreds of machines and the equivalent of 8,400 MIPS-years.

The dramatic speedup comes from both faster hardware and improved algorithms. This is why security standards have long since moved to **2048-bit and 4096-bit keys** — 512-bit RSA is considered completely broken for any serious use.
