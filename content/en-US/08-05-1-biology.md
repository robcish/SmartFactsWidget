Tardigrades (water bears) are microscopic animals that can survive **extreme conditions**: temperatures from −272°C to 150°C, pressures 6 times greater than the deepest ocean, and **radiation** hundreds of times above lethal human doses. In 2007, they became the first animals to **survive exposure to outer space.**
