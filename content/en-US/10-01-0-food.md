title: Caffeine blocks the brain's sleep signal
teaser: It does not create energy directly; it mostly prevents adenosine from making you feel tired.

As the day goes on, adenosine builds up in the brain and promotes sleepiness. Caffeine fits into some of the same receptors, so the signal is muted and you feel more alert.

That is why coffee often works best against fatigue rather than as a true source of extra energy.
