title: Trademarks can outlive copyrights
teaser: A brand mark may last indefinitely if it stays in active use.

Copyright usually runs for a fixed term and then expires. Trademark law works differently: protection can continue as long as a mark is still used in commerce and defended against confusing imitation.

That is why very old logos or product names can remain legally protected long after the original designers and copyright terms are gone.
