Early Olympic marathons did not all use the same length. In London in 1908, organizers set the course so it could start at Windsor Castle and finish in front of the royal box in the stadium.

That route measured 42.195 kilometers, and the distance eventually became the official standard. A choice made for one event ended up defining races around the world.
