Automatic weather stations on the Greenland Ice Sheet catch brief, brutal cold snaps. The **−69.6°C** reading on **22 December 1991** remains one of the lowest temperatures ever confirmed in the Northern Hemisphere—far below everyday winter chill in populated regions.
