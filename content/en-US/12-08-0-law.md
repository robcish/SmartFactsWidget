Maritime law cares how goods left a ship. **Flotsam** floats off a wreck on its own. **Jetsam** was thrown overboard to lighten the vessel. **Lagan** is sunk but marked, often with a buoy, because someone still claims it. **Derelict** is abandoned with no hope of recovery.

Those labels decide salvage rights and ownership fights. A romantic “treasure on the tide” is, to a court, a very specific kind of lost property.
