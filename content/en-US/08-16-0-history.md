Martha Washington's portrait appeared on the **1896 US one-dollar silver certificate**, making her the only woman ever depicted on American paper money. The certificate was part of the famous "Educational Series," prized by collectors for its elaborate allegorical artwork.

The Treasury chose her likeness partly to honor the role of First Ladies, yet **no woman has appeared on US paper currency since**. Efforts to feature women on bills — such as Harriet Tubman on the $20 — have been proposed but repeatedly delayed.
