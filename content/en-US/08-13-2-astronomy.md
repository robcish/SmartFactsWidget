Published anonymously in **1827** by University of Virginia professor George Tucker, *A Voyage to the Moon* introduced a fictional metal called "lunarium" that repels Earth's gravity. The protagonist coats his spacecraft with it and floats to the Moon — no rockets needed.

This predates Jules Verne's *From the Earth to the Moon* (1865) by nearly four decades and H.G. Wells' gravity-blocking "cavorite" in *The First Men in the Moon* (1901) by over 70 years. Tucker's novel marks the transition from magical or divine space travel in literature to pseudo-scientific explanations — an early step toward hard science fiction.
