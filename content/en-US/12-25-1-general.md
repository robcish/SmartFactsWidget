In the 1840s **Tom Smith** sold French-style sugared almonds in paper twists. He later copied the crackle of a log on the fire with a tiny strip of chemically treated card, and the **Christmas cracker** was born.

Hats, mottoes, and plastic trinkets piled in afterward. The bang is still a bit of theatre: two people pull, a fulminate snap fires, and a table gets a few seconds of shared surprise.
