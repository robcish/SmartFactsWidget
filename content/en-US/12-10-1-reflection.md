The Stoic teacher **Epictetus**, once enslaved in Rome, told students to sort every concern: some things are **up to us** (our judgments, aims, and refusals), and some are not (the body, reputation, other people’s choices). Peace, in his view, begins with that sorting.

The advice is stern rather than cozy. You still act in the world. You simply stop treating luck and other minds as if they were extra limbs.
