Under very specific temperature and pressure conditions, water can exist as ice, liquid, and vapor at the same time. Scientists call that balance the triple point.

It sounds impossible because everyday experience keeps boiling and freezing far apart. Physics becomes more surprising when the environment is controlled precisely enough.
