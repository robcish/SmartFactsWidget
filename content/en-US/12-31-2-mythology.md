**Janus** was the two-faced god of gates, keys, and beginnings. Romans invoked him at thresholds and at the start of enterprises, because a doorway is always two directions at once.

**January** takes his name. On the year's last night the image still fits: one face on the year that is ending, one on the year that has not yet been walked through.
