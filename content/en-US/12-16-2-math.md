Take a strip of paper, twist one end halfway, and tape it into a loop. The result is a **Möbius strip**: it has one face and one edge, unlike an ordinary bracelet of paper.

Cut it along the centerline and you do not get two rings; you get a longer loop with two twists. Topology cares about connection, not about how flat the paper looks on a table.
