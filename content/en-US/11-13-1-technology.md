QR codes store more than black squares in a grid. They also include error-correction data, often based on Reed-Solomon coding, so scanners can reconstruct missing information.

That is why a code may still work after being scratched, partly covered, or printed on curved packaging. Good design turns a fragile pattern into a surprisingly resilient one.
