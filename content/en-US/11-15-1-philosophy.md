The Ship of Theseus asks a deceptively simple question about identity. If an object is repaired piece by piece until none of the original material remains, is it still the original thing?

The puzzle matters far beyond boats. It touches bodies, memories, institutions, and even software, where continuity can feel real despite constant replacement underneath.
