In Shinto myth, the sun goddess **Amaterasu** fled into the heavenly rock cave after her brother Susanoo’s violence. Without her light, the High Plain of Heaven and the world below fell into darkness.

The other kami gathered, set out a mirror, and staged a noisy dance. Curiosity drew Amaterasu to the entrance; a strong god pulled her out and sealed the cave. Daylight returned, and the story still sits at the heart of Japan’s imperial sun lineage.
