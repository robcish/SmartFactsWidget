Lee Sedol retired from professional Go in 2019 and said that artificial intelligence had changed the game permanently. His comments were striking because he was also the person who proved AlphaGo was **not completely invincible**.

That contrast makes his story reflective rather than tragic. A human found the system's weakness once, yet still concluded that the wider direction of the game now belonged to machines and to players learning from them.
