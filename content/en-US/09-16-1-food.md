Dashi is a classic example of **umami synergy**. Kombu contributes glutamate, while bonito flakes add nucleotides such as inosinate, and together they create a stronger savory impression than either ingredient can produce on its own.

That is why some famous food pairings feel almost magically balanced. Good cooking sometimes depends on chemistry hiding inside tradition.
