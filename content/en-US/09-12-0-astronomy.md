The 1875 ascent of the **Zénith** became one of the most famous balloon disasters of the 19th century. When the crew climbed to extreme altitude, several men lost consciousness from lack of oxygen.

A memorial to two of the victims depicts them holding hands, giving the accident an unusually intimate afterlife in public memory. It links early flight not just with daring, but with the real risks of exploring the upper atmosphere.
