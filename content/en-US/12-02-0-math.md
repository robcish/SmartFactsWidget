G. H. Hardy once told Srinivasa Ramanujan that his taxi was number **1729**, “rather a dull number.” Ramanujan answered at once that it is the smallest number that is a sum of two positive cubes in **two different ways**: 1³ + 12³ and 9³ + 10³.

Numbers with that property are now called **taxicab numbers**. The story is a reminder that a plain integer can hide a tidy structure if you know where to look.
