By the mid-2020s, commercially available processors had climbed beyond **90 billion transistors**. Early integrated circuits began with counts so small that the modern figures feel almost mythical by comparison.

The contrast is one reason Moore's Law became such a cultural symbol. It turned invisible miniaturization into a story of staggering numbers that ordinary people could still grasp.
