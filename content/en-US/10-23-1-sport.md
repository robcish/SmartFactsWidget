Early Olympic marathons did not all use the same distance. The now-standard **42.195 kilometers** became famous after the 1908 London race, whose course was adjusted so it could start at Windsor Castle and finish in front of the royal box.

A practical event detail eventually became one of the most fixed numbers in world sport.
