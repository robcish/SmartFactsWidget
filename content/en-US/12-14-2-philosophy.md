In a famous passage of the **Zhuangzi**, the sage dreams he is a butterfly, happy and aimless. On waking he cannot tell whether he is Zhuang Zhou who dreamed of being a butterfly, or a butterfly now dreaming it is Zhou.

The story is not a riddle with a punchline. It loosens the habit of treating the waking self as the only real one, and asks how we know which side of a dream is the ground.
