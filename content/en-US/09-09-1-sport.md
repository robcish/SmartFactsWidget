Oscar Swahn became the **oldest Olympic gold medalist** when he won in running deer shooting at the 1912 Stockholm Games. He was 64, an age at which most Olympic careers are long over.

His record survives because some events depend less on sprinting power and more on control, timing, and concentration. It is a reminder that athletic excellence does not always peak young.
