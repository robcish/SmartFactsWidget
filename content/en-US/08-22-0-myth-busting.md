In **2026**, a magnetic tape containing **Unix Version 4** (1973) was found at the University of Utah and its contents were successfully recovered. It is the earliest known surviving distribution of this version.

Before this discovery, Unix V4 was thought to be entirely lost — no source code or binaries had been preserved. The find gave historians their first direct look at the transitional version where Unix was being **rewritten from assembly into C**, a pivotal moment in computing history.
