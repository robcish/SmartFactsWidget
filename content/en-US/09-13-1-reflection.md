title: A transistor once fit in a single atom

Researchers at the University of New South Wales demonstrated a **single-atom transistor** by positioning a phosphorus atom inside a silicon crystal and controlling current through it. It was a striking symbol of how far miniaturization had gone.

The result also raises a reflective question about limits. When devices approach atomic scales, future progress depends less on simple shrinking and more on entirely new ways of engineering computation.
