When foods rich in **glutamate** (like Parmesan cheese or tomatoes) are combined with those containing **nucleotides** like IMP or GMP (found in dried bonito or shiitake mushrooms), the perceived umami intensity can be **up to eight times stronger** than the sum of the individual ingredients.

This synergistic effect explains why classic flavor combinations work so well — Japanese dashi combines kombu (glutamate) with bonito flakes (IMP), and Italian cuisine pairs tomatoes with Parmesan. Chefs have exploited this principle for centuries, long before science identified the mechanism.
