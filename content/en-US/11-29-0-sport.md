When James Naismith invented basketball in 1891, players threw the ball into peach baskets attached high on a gym wall. At first, the bottoms stayed in place, so someone had to retrieve the ball after every score.

Only later did the equipment evolve into open hoops with nets. A global sport started with a very literal adaptation of whatever containers were available.
