IBM's chess computer was originally called **"Deep Thought"**, after the fictional computer in *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy*. However, IBM's marketing department realized the name too closely resembled **"Deep Throat"** — both the Watergate informant and the notorious 1972 film.

The machine was renamed **"Deep Blue"**, combining "deep" (referencing the depth of its search algorithm) with "Big Blue" (IBM's longtime nickname). The rebranding proved fortunate — "Deep Blue" became one of the most recognized names in computing history.
