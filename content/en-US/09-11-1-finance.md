Moore's Law is often described as a **self-fulfilling economic roadmap**. Semiconductor companies spent enormous sums on fabrication, design, and research partly because the industry expected steady transistor growth.

In that sense, the forecast helped finance its own future. Once customers, investors, and engineers planned around the curve, falling behind carried real commercial risk.
