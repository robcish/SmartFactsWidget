title: Pleasure fades faster than we expect
teaser: Psychologists call it hedonic adaptation: good news quickly becomes normal.

People are often sure that a new job, purchase, or milestone will change their happiness for a long time. In practice, we usually adapt quickly, and yesterday's thrilling upgrade becomes today's baseline.

That does not make achievements meaningless. It simply means that attention, relationships, and habits often shape well-being more than any single reward.

[Hedonic treadmill (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hedonic_treadmill)
