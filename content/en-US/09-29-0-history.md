People had used pencils and separate erasers for a long time, but the combination became official when Hymen Lipman patented a pencil with an eraser attached at one end. It is a small invention that feels inevitable only in hindsight.

The design succeeded because it removed one tiny inconvenience from everyday writing, which is often how durable tools win.
