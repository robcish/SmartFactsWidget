Before widespread street lighting, urban nights were far darker and riskier. Gas lamps and later electric lights did more than improve visibility: they changed working hours, policing, transport, and the idea that public life could continue after sunset.

A technical upgrade ended up reshaping daily rhythms for entire cities.
