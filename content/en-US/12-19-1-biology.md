The plasmodial slime mold **Physarum polycephalum** is a single giant cell that spreads as a yellow network. When food sits at two points in a maze, the organism withdraws from dead ends and thickens the shortest connecting tubes.

Researchers have even used it as a living analog computer for transport maps. Intelligence here is not a skull; it is a body that explores, then prunes.
