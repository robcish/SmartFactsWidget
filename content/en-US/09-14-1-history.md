Scholars have long pointed out the pattern of **multiple independent discovery**. When the same tools, questions, and methods spread through a period, different researchers can reach similar breakthroughs without copying one another.

That makes the history of science less like a parade of lone geniuses and more like a landscape of shared conditions. Some ideas become "ready" for discovery.
