In Old Norse mythology, **Bifrost** connects Midgard, the human world, with Asgard, the realm of the gods. Medieval sources describe it as shimmering and beautiful, but also blazing with fire so hostile forces cannot cross it easily.

That mix of beauty and danger helps explain why the bridge became one of the most memorable images in Norse myth.
