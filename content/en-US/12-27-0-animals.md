**Pistol shrimp** (family Alpheidae) cock one oversized claw and snap it shut. The motion fires a jet of water that forms a **cavitation bubble**. When the bubble collapses, it releases a bang loud enough to stun small fish and crabs.

For a split second the collapse can even flash faint light. The weapon is physics in a claw: not a punch, but a controlled underwater implosion.
