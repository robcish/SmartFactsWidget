Saffron is made from the red stigmas of the saffron crocus, and each flower provides only three of them. Harvesting is delicate, mostly done by hand, and large numbers of blossoms are needed for even a small amount of spice.

That labor is a big reason saffron has long been one of the world's most valuable culinary ingredients.
