Learn a rare surname or buy a red car, and the same thing appears on every street. Psychologists call this the **frequency illusion** (sometimes the Baader–Meinhof phenomenon). First, **selective attention** tags the item as interesting. Then **confirmation bias** notices each new hit and forgets the misses.

The world did not fill with copies overnight. Your filter did. Knowing the trick does not stop it, but it can keep a coincidence from feeling like a hidden message.
