Linux gradually displaced every rival operating system in high-performance computing. Since 2017, **all Top500 supercomputers** have used Linux.

That dominance comes from flexibility: research teams can tune kernels, drivers, and schedulers for specialized hardware instead of waiting for a closed vendor roadmap.
