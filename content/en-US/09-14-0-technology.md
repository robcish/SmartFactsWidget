In 2021, IBM unveiled a **2 nanometer test chip** with about 50 billion transistors on an area roughly the size of a fingernail. The headline number was not just about scale, but about how precisely modern fabrication can place and shape structures.

Announcements like that matter because they preview techniques that later influence real products. They are snapshots of what the next manufacturing era might make practical.
