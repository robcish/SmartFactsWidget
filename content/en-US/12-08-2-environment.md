title: Sahara dust feeds the Amazon

Heavy rain would strip Amazon soils of nutrients if nothing replaced them. Satellite and field studies show that plumes of **Saharan dust**, especially from Chad’s **Bodélé Depression**, cross the Atlantic carrying **phosphorus** and other minerals.

The same dusty air can seed Atlantic clouds and affect hurricane seasons. A desert basin and a rainforest are linked by a river of wind.
