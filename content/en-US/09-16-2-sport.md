The marathon was not always 42.195 kilometers. That now-standard distance traces back to the **1908 London Olympics**, where the course was adjusted so the race could start at Windsor Castle and finish in front of the royal box.

Later, the length became official and turned one local arrangement into a global rule. An arbitrary event-day choice ended up defining one of the world's most famous races.
