Memory is not a perfect recording that stays untouched in storage. Each time we recall an experience, the memory can become active again and may be slightly updated before it settles back down.

This process helps explain why confidence and accuracy do not always match. A vivid memory can still contain details reshaped by later conversations, feelings, or repeated retelling.
