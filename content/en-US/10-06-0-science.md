The Mpemba effect does not happen in every freezer or every experiment, but it has been observed often enough to remain scientifically interesting. Evaporation, convection, dissolved gases, and container conditions can all matter.

It is a good reminder that simple-seeming systems can hide surprisingly tangled physics.
