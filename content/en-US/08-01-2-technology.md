When **US Cyber Command** unveiled its emblem in 2009, observers noticed a ring of 32 hexadecimal characters around the inner gold seal. It turned out to be the **MD5 hash** of the command's mission statement.

The hash — `9ec4c12949a4f31474f299058ce2b22a` — was a deliberate design choice, a cryptographic wink aimed at the tech-savvy community USCYBERCOM serves. It also became an unintentional commentary on the weakness of MD5 itself: anyone with the mission text could verify the hash in seconds, making it a **fingerprint**, not a secret.
