title: The Great Wall isn't moon-visible
teaser: The famous claim sounds impressive, but the Great Wall is far too narrow to stand out from the Moon.
published: true
version: 1
---
The Great Wall of China is enormous in length, but not in width. From the Moon it would be much too thin to resolve with the naked eye, especially because its color blends into the surrounding landscape.

Astronauts in low Earth orbit can sometimes spot sections under favorable conditions, but that is very different from seeing it from lunar distance. The myth survived because it flatters human intuition more than optics.
