title: Most languages include nasal sounds

Nasal consonants such as **m**, **n**, and **ng** appear in the overwhelming majority of the world's languages. They are easy to produce because air can flow through the nose while the mouth shapes the sound.

A few languages are famous for lacking ordinary nasal consonants, which makes them linguistically interesting precisely because they are unusual. The pattern is a good reminder that some speech sounds are close to global defaults.
