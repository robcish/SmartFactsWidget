Programmer **Bob Bemer** recognized the two-digit year problem as early as 1958 and spent years trying to get major institutions to care. His story is a reminder that technical warnings often arrive decades before anyone budgets to fix them.

There is something reflective in that timeline: a problem can be real, documented, and still ignored simply because it belongs to the future rather than to the current quarter.
