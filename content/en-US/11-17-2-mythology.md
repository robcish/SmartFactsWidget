Pegasus does not enter Greek myth through a quiet stable door. In the best-known version, the winged horse bursts forth when Perseus kills Medusa, emerging together with the warrior Chrysaor.

The birth is grotesque and luminous at once, which is typical of Greek myth. Beauty, terror, and transformation often arrive in the same scene.
