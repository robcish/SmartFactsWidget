In **1994**, Peter Shor described a quantum algorithm for factoring large numbers much faster than known classical methods. That result gave quantum computing a concrete mathematical use-case with huge consequences for cryptography, especially **RSA**. It is one of the clearest examples of abstract math reshaping technology strategy.
