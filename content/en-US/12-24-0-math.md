Rotate the curve **1/x** from 1 to infinity and you get **Gabriel's horn** (Torricelli's trumpet). The volume converges to a finite number, π, while the surface area grows without bound.

The usual riddle is that you could fill the horn with a finite amount of paint, yet never coat the inside. The catch is that mathematical “paint” of zero thickness is not house paint; the paradox is about limits, not decorating.
