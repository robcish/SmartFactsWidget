In December **2020**, a team led by Pan Jianwei at the University of Science and Technology of China demonstrated **Jiuzhang** — a photonic quantum computer that used 76 detected photons to perform Gaussian boson sampling. The calculation took about 200 seconds.

The fastest classical supercomputer would need an estimated **600 million years** for the same task. Unlike Google's Sycamore (which used superconducting qubits at near absolute zero), Jiuzhang operates at room temperature using light particles. It was the first quantum advantage demonstration that didn't rely on superconducting technology, proving that multiple hardware approaches can achieve quantum supremacy.
