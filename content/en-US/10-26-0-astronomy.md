Laser ranging shows that the Moon moves about **3.8 centimeters** farther from Earth each year. The cause is tidal friction: Earth's spin transfers angular momentum to the Moon's orbit. Over geological time, that tiny drift adds up to a dramatically different Earth-Moon relationship.
