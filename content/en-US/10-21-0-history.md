Popular retellings often imagine the **Library of Alexandria** vanishing in a single catastrophic blaze. Historians more often describe a long decline shaped by political upheaval, lost funding, and damage across different periods.

That makes the story less cinematic, but more realistic: important institutions often disappear through neglect as much as through disaster.
