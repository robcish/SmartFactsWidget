On **26 June 1974**, a cashier at Marsh Supermarket in Troy, Ohio, scanned a pack of Wrigley’s Juicy Fruit. That swipe is remembered as the first commercial use of the Universal Product Code at checkout.

The stripes were designed so a laser could read a number even if the pack was slightly tilted. A stick of gum was a humble launch for a system that now prices almost everything on a shelf.
