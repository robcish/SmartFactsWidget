In the sixteenth century, rich silver mines at **Joachimsthal** (today Jáchymov) produced heavy coins called Joachimsthalers, soon shortened to **thalers**. Dutch *daalder* and English *dollar* are later cousins of that name.

The coin's size and reputation traveled farther than the valley. When Spanish pieces of eight and later New World currencies needed an English label, **dollar** was already waiting as a familiar word for a hefty silver piece.
