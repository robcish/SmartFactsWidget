title: Endings shape memory more than averages

Psychologists describe a **peak-end rule**: when people look back on an event, they often remember the most intense moment and the ending more strongly than the average of everything in between.

That helps explain why a stressful day can still feel good in memory if it ended warmly, or why one bad finish can sour an otherwise decent experience.
