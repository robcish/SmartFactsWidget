Discovered in Rome, the **Zoninus collar** is a bronze ring inscribed in Latin: *"Hold me, lest I flee, and return me to my master Zoninus — you will receive a gold coin."* It is the only known Roman slave collar that offers a **cash reward** for the wearer's return.

Such collars were riveted around the necks of enslaved people considered flight risks. Most bore only the owner's name and a plea to detain. The Zoninus collar's added bounty suggests its owner either had an especially determined runaway — or placed an unusually high value on that particular person.
