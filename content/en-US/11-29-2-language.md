Pāṇini's grammar of Sanskrit is famous for its compact, highly systematic rules. Written in ancient India, it shows an analytical precision that still earns admiration from linguists today.

His work reminds us that sophisticated language study did not begin in the modern university. People were building powerful models of grammar more than two millennia ago.
