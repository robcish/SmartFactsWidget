title: Frogs often eat their own shed skin

Frogs regularly shed the outer layer of skin in a process called **sloughing**. Instead of leaving it behind, many species pull the loosened skin toward the mouth with their legs and eat it.

That habit may help them recover nutrients and keep the skin surface in working order. It also means they leave less obvious biological litter in places where predators could notice them.
