AlphaGo Zero started with only the rules of Go and improved by playing against itself, without studying human games. After about **three days** of self-play, it had already defeated the earlier AlphaGo version that beat Lee Sedol.

That result mattered because it showed that strong search combined with learning can discover its own patterns instead of copying expert examples. In other words, the system built a powerful Go intuition from mathematics and repetition rather than from human tutoring.
