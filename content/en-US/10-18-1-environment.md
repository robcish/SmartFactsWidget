Mangrove forests grow where land and sea constantly meet. Their dense roots help stabilize shorelines, reduce erosion, and weaken incoming waves during storms.

They also store significant amounts of **blue carbon** in waterlogged soils, which makes them valuable not just for biodiversity but for climate protection as well.
