American Sign Language, Polish Sign Language, and other signed systems are **independent natural languages**. Their grammar can rely on handshape, motion, facial expression, and use of space rather than on sound.

That is why a signed language is not simply a spoken language transferred into gestures. It develops its own structure, history, and regional variation within Deaf communities.
