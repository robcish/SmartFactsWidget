**E Ink** displays use microcapsules filled with charged black and white pigment. An electric field pulls one color to the surface and the other away, so the letters stay put until the next page turn.

Because the image is reflected light, not a glowing panel, a paragraph can remain on screen using almost no power. The trick is electrophoresis, borrowed from chemistry and scaled into a paperback-like slab.
