title: Honey can last for years

Honey is naturally hard for bacteria and molds to colonize. It contains very little water, plenty of sugar, and acids that make spoilage much less likely.

Archaeologists have even found ancient honey in sealed vessels that remained recognizable after centuries. Crystallization may change the texture, but it does not mean the honey has gone bad.
