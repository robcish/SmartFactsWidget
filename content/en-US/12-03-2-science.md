**Aerogel** is a gel whose liquid has been replaced with gas without collapsing the structure. Silica versions are often more than **99% air**, which is why they look misty and weigh so little. Touch one and it can feel fragile, yet the nanoscale network is stiff enough to support many times its own mass.

NASA used aerogel on the **Stardust** mission to catch comet dust at high speed without vaporizing it. The same family of materials makes superb thermal insulation.
