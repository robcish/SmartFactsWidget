Chroniclers of **Strasbourg** described a **dancing plague** in 1518. A woman named Frau Troffea began to dance in the street; within weeks, dozens more joined, some until they fell from exhaustion.

Officials first tried more music, hoping the fit would burn out. Later they banned public dancing and sent sufferers to a shrine. Historians still argue over ergot, stress, and social contagion, but the civic records are real.
