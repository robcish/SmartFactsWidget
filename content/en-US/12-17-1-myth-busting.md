title: The tongue map is a classroom leftover

Twentieth-century textbooks often showed a map: sweet on the tip, bitter at the back, sour and salty on the sides. That diagram grew from a misreading of older German research and was copied more faithfully than the data deserved.

Later work found receptors for the basic tastes across the tongue, with only modest differences in sensitivity. A colorful poster is not a wiring diagram of flavor.
