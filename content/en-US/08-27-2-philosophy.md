The **Ship of Theseus** is a thought experiment from ancient Greece: if you gradually replace **every plank** of a wooden ship, is the resulting vessel the same ship? And if you rebuild the original from the old planks, which one is the "real" ship? After 2,500 years, philosophers still disagree on the answer.
