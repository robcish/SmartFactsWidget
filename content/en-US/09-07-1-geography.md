The Y2K problem was not just a story about giant mainframes. It also disrupted **local hardware in specific regions**, including police breathalyzers in Hong Kong and taxi meters in Jiangsu, China.

Those failures are a good reminder that software bugs travel through geography. The same date rollover could create different practical problems depending on what machines a city relied on.
