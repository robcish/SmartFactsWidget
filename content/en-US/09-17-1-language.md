Despite the shared spoken language background of the UK and US, **British Sign Language** and **American Sign Language** developed along different historical lines.

ASL was shaped in part by French Sign Language, while BSL belongs to a separate sign-language family. Sign languages follow their own histories, not the borders of spoken English.
