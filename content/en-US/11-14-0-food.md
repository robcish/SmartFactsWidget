Vanilla is derived from orchids of the genus Vanilla, especially Vanilla planifolia. The long pods are often called beans, but botanically they are fruit capsules from a flowering vine.

Outside the plant's native range, growers usually pollinate the flowers by hand because the original pollinators are absent. That labor helps explain why real vanilla is one of the most expensive spices in the world.
