Before Apollo crews walked on the Moon, NASA trained them with the **Reduced Gravity Walking Simulator**. The setup tilted astronauts sideways and supported part of their weight so their movements would mimic lunar gravity.

Because of that strange arrangement, trainees sometimes looked as if they were walking on walls. It was an ingenious Earthbound trick for rehearsing a world where every step, hop, and stumble would behave differently.
