The 1954 Yongdusan fire swept through central Busan and erased a remarkable part of Korea's royal record. Among the losses were **30 state portraits** of Joseon kings, objects used in court ritual as well as remembrance.

Because royal portraits were copied only in limited numbers, each loss mattered. The fire became a reminder that archives can survive centuries of conflict yet still vanish in a single modern disaster.
