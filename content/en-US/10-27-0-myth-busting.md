The claim that the **Great Wall of China** is the one human-made object visible from space is a popular myth. In reality, visibility depends on altitude, weather, lighting, and contrast with the surroundings, and the Wall is often hard to distinguish.

Cities, roads, and airports can be easier to notice under the right conditions.
