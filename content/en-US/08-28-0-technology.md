Lee Sedol's move 78 in the fourth AlphaGo match became known as the **"divine move"** because it landed where the system's policy network had assigned almost no probability. AlphaGo suddenly had to search a line it had not prepared for.

That surprise did not make the program useless, but it exposed a real limit of early game-playing AI: when its learned intuition missed a rare idea, the rest of the system could wobble with it. Sedol went on to win the game, the only official AlphaGo defeat against a human player.
