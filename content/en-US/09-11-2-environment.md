Rock's Law, sometimes called Moore's second law, says the price of a leading semiconductor fab tends to **double every few years**. Faster chips do not arrive by math alone; they require bigger buildings, cleaner rooms, and more complex tools.

That makes computing progress an environmental story as well as a technical one. Each manufacturing leap depends on more energy, more materials, and more industrial infrastructure.
