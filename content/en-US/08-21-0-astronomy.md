In the constellation Cancer, roughly **1.06 billion light-years** away, astronomers discovered **SDSS J0849+1114** — a system where three galaxies are merging and their supermassive black holes are spiraling toward one another.

Triple black hole mergers are extremely rare. This system is one of the tightest such configurations ever observed, with all three nuclei separated by less than **10,000 light-years**. The eventual merger will produce gravitational waves far more complex than those from binary black hole collisions.
