The hash function now called **SHA-0** was published by NIST in **1993** as a US federal standard. Less than two years later it was replaced by **SHA-1** after the NSA made a technical revision that was never fully explained in public. That unusual retreat made SHA-0 a small but famous dead end in cryptography history.
