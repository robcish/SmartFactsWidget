For decades tennis was played with white balls. As matches moved onto color television, viewers and players lost the ball against pale clothing and court lines. In the **1970s** a high-visibility yellow, later nicknamed optic yellow, spread through professional play.

Wimbledon kept white longer than many tournaments, then switched too. A small change in pigment was really a change in who the sport was designed to be watched by.
