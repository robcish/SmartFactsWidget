A **sexagesimal** system uses 60 as its base. It is handy because 60 divides evenly by 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6, so fractions of a circle or a day stay as whole numbers more often than in base 10.

We inherited the habit as **60 minutes**, **60 seconds**, and **360 degrees**. The cuneiform tablets are gone from daily life; the arithmetic of the sky calendar is not.
