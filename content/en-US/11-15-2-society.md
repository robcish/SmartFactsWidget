The number 999 was launched in London so callers could reach help quickly without needing local knowledge. It is widely regarded as the first dedicated emergency telephone number anywhere in the world.

The system grew out of a tragedy: a fire in Wimpole Street after which relatives reported they could not get urgent help fast enough. One simple number changed how public safety could be organized at scale.
