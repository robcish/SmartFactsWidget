A set of rare **Buddhist silk paintings** from the Sinheungsa temple in Gangwon Province, South Korea, turned up thousands of miles from home — in an attic in the small town of Hopkinton, New Hampshire. They are believed to date from the Joseon Dynasty.

How they got there remains unclear, but they likely left Korea during the Japanese occupation or the Korean War. The paintings are significant because very few silk-scroll works from Gangwon Province survive, making this accidental discovery an important find for Korean art history.
