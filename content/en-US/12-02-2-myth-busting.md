title: Chameleons do not hide by color
teaser: Their famous shifts are mainly social signals and temperature, not camouflage.
published: true
version: 1
---
Cartoons treat chameleons as walking camouflage kits. Real color change is driven mostly by **mood, rivalry, courtship, and heat**. Layers of cells called chromatophores expand or contract, flashing greens, yellows, and dark tones.

Camouflage can play a part, but it is not the main trick. Many species already match their habitat at rest. Watching a male brighten at a rival tells you more about **communication** than about disappearing into a leaf.
