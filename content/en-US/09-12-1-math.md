Moore once joked that **"Moore's law is a violation of Murphy's law"** because things kept getting better instead of worse. Beneath the humor was a real point: exponential improvement over decades is rare and hard to maintain.

The math behind the curve is simple to state but brutal to deliver. Every new generation demanded tighter manufacturing, better design tools, and fewer mistakes across a huge supply chain.
