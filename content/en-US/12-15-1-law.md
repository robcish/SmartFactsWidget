The **Outer Space Treaty**, which entered into force in 1967, says outer space, including the Moon and other celestial bodies, is not subject to national appropriation by claim of sovereignty, occupation, or any other means.

Flags and footprints can still be historic. They do not create a deed. The same pact also limits weapons of mass destruction in orbit and frames space as a domain for peaceful exploration.
