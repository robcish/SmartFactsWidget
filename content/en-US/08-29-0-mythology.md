When world champion **Ke Jie** lost to AlphaGo in 2017, he said it felt as if the machine had revealed flaws in human Go tradition. His reaction captured how strange AlphaGo's style looked even to the best players alive.

The remark was not just frustration. AlphaGo often chose moves that first seemed mysterious, then later looked elegant and strong, almost like a new mythology of the game had appeared overnight.
