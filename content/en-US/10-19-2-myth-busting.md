title: Vikings did not wear horned helmets
teaser: The famous horned Viking helmet is mostly a modern stage and museum fantasy.

---
Archaeological finds from the Viking Age do not support the image of warriors fighting in horned helmets. The stereotype became popular much later, especially through **19th-century art and opera costume design**.

Real combat helmets had to be practical, and large horns would have made them heavier and easier to grab.
