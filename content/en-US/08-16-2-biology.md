Plants can channel up to **30% of the carbon** they produce through photosynthesis to mycorrhizal fungi living on their roots. In return, these fungal networks supply the plants with nitrogen and phosphorus that would otherwise be inaccessible.

This underground exchange is not one-sided charity — it functions more like a **biological marketplace** where both partners adjust their contributions based on supply and demand. The fungal threads can extend meters beyond the root zone, vastly expanding a plant's effective reach.
