Honey is one of the only foods that **never spoils**. Archaeologists have found pots of honey in **Egyptian tombs** over 3,000 years old that were still perfectly edible. Honey's longevity comes from its low moisture content, high acidity, and natural production of **hydrogen peroxide.**
