Fresh pineapple contains **bromelain**, a mix of enzymes that cut protein chains. Cooks use that chemistry to soften meat, but the same action is why a raw chunk can leave your mouth feeling slightly scraped.

Heat and canning disable much of the enzyme, so tinned rings behave more politely in jelly. The fruit is both dessert and a mild, edible laboratory.
