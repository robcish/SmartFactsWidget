By **1900**, women made up **38% of university students** in Wales, a remarkably high proportion for the era. This was partly because the University of Wales had admitted women from its founding in **1893**, unlike Oxford and Cambridge which restricted full degrees for women until 1920 and 1948 respectively.

Wales' progressive stance was driven by a strong nonconformist culture that valued education for all, and by the fact that Welsh universities were newer and less bound by centuries of tradition. The figure would not be matched by England until decades later.
