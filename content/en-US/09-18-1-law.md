In many legal systems, a defendant must be treated as **innocent until proven guilty**. That principle is meant to protect people from punishment based on rumor, fear, or political pressure.

It also shifts the burden of proof to the prosecution. Courts may differ on details, but the basic idea is that the state must justify its accusation.
