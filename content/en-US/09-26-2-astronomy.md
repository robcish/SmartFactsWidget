Distance alone does not guarantee visibility. Many nearby stars are dim red dwarfs, so only **a minority** shine brightly enough for naked-eye observers on Earth.

That is why the nearest stellar neighborhood contains many objects astronomy depends on, even though most people never notice them in the night sky.
