**Jai alai**, a form of Basque pelota, uses a curved basket called a **cesta** to catch and whip the ball against a granite wall. The ball is small, hard, and built to rebound, which is why the sport is often listed among the fastest ball games.

Frontons once packed crowds from the Basque Country to Florida. Helmets and rules tightened as speeds rose. The spectacle is still simple: one throw, a brutal bounce, and almost no time to think.
