After Deep Blue's historic victory over Kasparov in **1997**, IBM immediately **retired and dismantled** the machine, refusing Kasparov's repeated demands for a rematch. The match prize was **$700,000 for IBM** and $400,000 for Kasparov.

IBM's decision was strategic — they had achieved their publicity goal and feared a rematch loss would erase the gain. Kasparov accused IBM of cheating, claiming human grandmasters had intervened during games. IBM denied the allegations but never allowed independent verification, fueling conspiracy theories that persist to this day.
