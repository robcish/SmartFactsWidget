At the Mexico City Olympics, **Dick Fosbury** won gold by sprinting in a curve and clearing the bar **back-first**, landing on his shoulders. Coaches had preferred the straddle. Foam pits made a safer landing possible, so jumpers could experiment.

The **Fosbury flop** lets the athlete’s center of mass pass close to, or even below, the bar while the body arches over it. Within a few Games almost every finalist used some version of the same odd-looking leap.
