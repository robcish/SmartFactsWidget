In October **2015**, Google DeepMind's **AlphaGo** defeated European Go champion Fan Hui **5–0** on a full 19×19 board without handicap — the first time any computer program achieved this feat. Go had long been considered far harder for computers than chess due to its vastly larger search space.

Unlike Deep Blue's brute-force approach, AlphaGo used **deep neural networks** trained through self-play and reinforcement learning. The victory was kept secret until published in *Nature* in January 2016, shocking the Go and AI communities simultaneously.
