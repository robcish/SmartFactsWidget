The **star-nosed mole** wears a ring of 22 fleshy rays packed with touch organs called **Eimer’s organs**. It is nearly blind, so it hunts by brushing the star through wet soil and water.

Lab work by Kenneth Catania found it can detect, decide, and eat a small prey item in as little as **227 milliseconds**, among the fastest feeding sequences measured in mammals. The star is a high-speed tactile map, not a decoration.
