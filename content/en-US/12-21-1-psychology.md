teaser: The spotlight effect makes ordinary slips feel like a public broadcast.

In classic studies, people who wore an embarrassing T-shirt later guessed that **far more classmates had noticed** than actually had. The mind treats our own awkward moments as a spotlight, while everyone else is busy starring in their own.

The bias is useful as a warning flare, but it overrates how closely strangers track us. A small stumble that loops in memory is often a blur to the room.

[Spotlight effect (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spotlight_effect)
