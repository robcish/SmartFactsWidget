**Archerfish** live in mangrove creeks and aim a mouth jet at insects above the water. They account for refraction, so the spit hits a target that looks bent from below.

A successful shot drops a meal within reach of a fast lunge. The hunt is marksmanship and physics in a few centimeters of brackish water.
