A **whale fall** is a dead whale that settles on the deep seafloor. First come scavengers such as sharks and hagfish. Later, bone-eating worms and microbes mine lipids locked in the skeleton.

In the dark, food usually arrives as a thin rain of debris. One large body is a banquet that can structure a local community for years, a reminder that even death can be habitat.
