During the solar eclipse of **1868**, astronomers including Pierre Janssen and Norman Lockyer studied the Sun’s chromosphere. They recorded a yellow spectral line that did not match any known terrestrial element.

Lockyer named the mystery **helium**, after the Greek sun god. Only in **1895** did William Ramsay isolate the gas from a uranium mineral on Earth. The periodic table gained an entry that sunlight announced before a laboratory did.
