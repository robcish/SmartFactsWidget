A story from 1919 claimed a child died after eating a **poinsettia**. Later reviews found no such medical case, yet the legend stuck to Christmas décor.

The plant's milky sap can **irritate** mouth and skin, so it is not a snack. Compared with true household poisons, though, poinsettias rank as a mild nuisance, not a lethal trap waiting under the tree.
