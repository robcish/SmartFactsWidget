During the first game of the **1997 rematch**, a bug in Deep Blue's evaluation code caused it to select a move essentially at random when it couldn't decide between options. Kasparov, stunned by the unexpected move, interpreted it as a sign of **deep strategic insight**.

The psychological impact was enormous. Kasparov later said the move haunted him throughout the match, contributing to his collapse in the decisive **Game 6**, which he resigned after just 19 moves. IBM's team only revealed the bug's role years later.
