Early Olympic marathons were not all the same length. The now-standard 42.195 kilometers became famous after the 1908 race in London, which was arranged to start at Windsor Castle and finish in front of the royal box.

That route turned a rough tradition into a precise global standard. A race inspired by legend ended up being defined by logistics and spectatorship.
