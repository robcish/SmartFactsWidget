On 3 December 1992, **Neil Papworth** sent **Merry Christmas** over Vodafone's new short-message service. The note went from a desktop computer to a colleague's handset, because phones could not yet type a reply.

SMS later became a billing unit, then a social habit, then a layer under apps. The first payload was not a stock tip or an emergency. It was a seasonal sentence small enough to fit in 160 characters.
