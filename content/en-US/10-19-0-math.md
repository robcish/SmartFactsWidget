title: Benford's law favors low digits

In collections like river lengths, city populations, and financial figures, leading digits are not evenly distributed. **Benford's law** predicts that about 30% of values begin with 1, while fewer than 5% begin with 9.

That pattern is so reliable that auditors sometimes use it to flag suspicious datasets that look too uniform to be natural.
