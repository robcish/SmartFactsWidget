Betty Holberton is often remembered for programming **ENIAC**, but she also helped shape practical data processing for early commercial machines. In the early UNIVAC era, she co-authored work on sorting large collections of records efficiently.

That mattered because mid-century computers were not just calculators. They were becoming information machines, and sorting payroll, census, and business data was one of the jobs that turned raw hardware into useful technology.
