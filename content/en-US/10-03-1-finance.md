According to the usual origin story, Frank McNamara forgot his wallet at a restaurant and imagined a card that would let customers settle the bill later. Diners Club soon turned that inconvenience into a business model.

Whether every detail is legendary or not, the episode captures how consumer finance often begins with ordinary friction.
