Cheetahs belong to the cat family, but they are not built like lions or tigers when it comes to vocal anatomy. Instead of producing a true roar, they make sounds such as **chirps, purrs, growls, and yelps**.

Those calls are useful for keeping track of cubs and coordinating at a distance on open ground.
