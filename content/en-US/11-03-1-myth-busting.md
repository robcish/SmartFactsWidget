title: Vikings did not battle in horned helmets
teaser: The iconic horned Viking look owes more to 19th-century costume design than archaeology.
published: true
version: 1
---
No reliable evidence shows Viking warriors wearing horned helmets in combat. The image spread through romantic art and opera staging, where dramatic silhouettes mattered more than practical protection.
