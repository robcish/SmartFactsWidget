Austin J. Tobin Plaza sat between the Twin Towers and held Fritz Koenig's sculpture **The Sphere**. After the September 11 attacks, the plaza was obliterated, yet the battered sculpture was recovered from the debris.

It was later moved to Battery Park, where its scars became part of its meaning. What began as public art for a business complex turned into a historical witness to one of the most documented events of the modern era.
