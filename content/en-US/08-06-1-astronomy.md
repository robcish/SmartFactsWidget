On 22 July 1951, two stray dogs named **Dezik** and **Tsygan** were launched aboard a Soviet R-1 rocket from the Kapustin Yar range. They reached an altitude of about 100 km — crossing the Kármán line into space — and returned safely by parachute.

The flight lasted only minutes, but it proved that living creatures could survive the forces of launch and brief weightlessness. Tsygan was later adopted by physicist Anatoli Blagonravov. Dezik flew again just a week later on a second mission, but tragically did not survive when the parachute failed.
