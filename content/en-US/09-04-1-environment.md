The 2015 **Logjam** attack was alarming not only because it targeted Diffie-Hellman, but because so much of the internet relied on the same small set of parameter groups. That kind of reuse meant one successful precomputation could threaten many systems at once.

It is a useful reminder that security has an environment as well as a math problem. When too many machines depend on the same defaults, weakness spreads through the whole ecosystem instead of staying local.
