The **bumblebee bat**, also called Kitti's hog-nosed bat, weighs roughly two grams and can fit easily in a human palm. Its body is tiny, but it still hunts insects on the wing like larger bats do.

Being so small brings trade-offs. The species lives in limestone caves in Thailand and Myanmar, and habitat disturbance can threaten entire local colonies very quickly.
