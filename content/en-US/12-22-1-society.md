The **Kula ring** links island communities of Papua New Guinea. Red-shell necklaces tend to travel one way around the circuit; white-shell armbands travel the other. A gift is meant to be held, shown, and later passed on, not parked as private stock.

Anthropologist Bronisław Malinowski described the system as a web of visits, reputation, and delayed return. Status grows from keeping the exchange alive, not from locking the ornaments in a chest.
