Plants linked by **mycorrhizal networks** can exchange carbon through fungi in the soil, but the traffic is not fixed. Studies have found that the direction of transfer can shift with season and life stage.

That means underground networks are less like a one-way pipeline and more like a dynamic trading system. Young seedlings, mature trees, and spring flowers can all become net donors or receivers at different times of year.
