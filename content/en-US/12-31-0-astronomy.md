Atomic clocks run smoother than the planet. When Earth's rotation drifts, timekeepers may add a **leap second**, creating a minute that counts to 60. Many of those extra ticks have landed on **New Year's Eve** UTC.

The last one so far was 31 December 2016. Officials now plan to retire the leap second in the 2030s because it complicates software. Until then, astronomy still peeks into civil time: the sky is not a quartz crystal.
