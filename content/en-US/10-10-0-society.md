For much of history, people did not share a standard two-day weekend. Rest days varied by religion, region, and the demands of agricultural or industrial work.

The Saturday-Sunday weekend became common only after labor movements, employers, and public institutions gradually settled on a schedule that fit both work rhythms and family life.
