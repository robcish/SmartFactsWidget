**Mono no aware** is often translated as “the pathos of things.” It is not gloom. It is a practiced attention to cherry blossoms, autumn light, or a conversation that will not come again, and the quiet feeling that their value is bound up with their ending.

The scholar Motoori Norinaga used the idea to read *The Tale of Genji*. Noticing transience can sharpen gratitude instead of rushing past the moment.
