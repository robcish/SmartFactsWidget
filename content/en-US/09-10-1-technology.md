The so-called **Y2K22 bug** struck Microsoft Exchange's malware filtering engine when it tried to parse a date-based version number that no longer fit the expected numeric range. As a result, some servers stopped processing email normally.

It was a very modern reminder of an old lesson: software often hides assumptions about calendars and numbering. When those assumptions break, even routine infrastructure can jam.
