A South Korean video store's software once calculated a rental as **100 years late**, producing an eye-popping fee of 8 million won. The glitch came from the Y2K date rollover, not from any real human mistake.

Cases like this are memorable because they reveal how strongly people trust printed totals and screens. Even an obviously impossible number can trigger embarrassment, stress, or panic before anyone checks the logic behind it.
