Paul Benioff's early work on the **quantum Turing machine** helped translate quantum mechanics into the language of computation. That mattered because it offered engineers and theorists a way to think about quantum systems as information-processing devices, not only as physics experiments. It became part of the conceptual bridge from theory to quantum hardware.
