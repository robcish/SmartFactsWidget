Most stones used in major curling events are cut from **Ailsa Craig** granite. Two textures from the same rock, often called Blue Hone and Common Green, are paired so the running surface and the striking band behave differently on ice.

The island's dense, low-porosity stone resists water, which keeps the sliding surface from pitting as quickly as ordinary granite. Harvesting is tightly limited, so a well-made stone can serve for decades.
