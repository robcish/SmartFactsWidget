Many cities adopted house numbering because administrations wanted to **count people more efficiently**. Officials could tax households, draft soldiers, and track residents more easily once every building had a stable identifier.

That means a familiar part of urban life began as a tool of state organization. What now feels ordinary was once a visible sign of expanding bureaucracy.
