"Null Island" is the playful name for coordinates **0°N, 0°E** in the Gulf of Guinea. It is not a real island, but cartographers and programmers use the term because bad location data often collapses to that exact point.

The joke stuck because it reveals something real about digital maps: when software lacks proper geographic information, the world can suddenly snap to a mathematically neat but humanly meaningless place.
