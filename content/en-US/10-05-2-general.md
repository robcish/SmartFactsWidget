title: Honey can last for years without spoiling
teaser: Low moisture and natural acidity make it a difficult home for microbes.
published: true
version: 1

Properly stored honey keeps extremely well because it contains little water, plenty of sugar, and compounds that discourage microbial growth. It may crystallize or change texture, but that is not the same thing as spoilage.

Its reputation for longevity is unusual enough that ancient samples have become famous.
