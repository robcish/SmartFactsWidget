Seahorses are famous because the **male becomes pregnant** in the practical sense: the female deposits eggs into his brood pouch, and he fertilizes and carries them until birth.

The arrangement flips many human expectations about reproduction, but it still follows the same biological logic of investment and survival. A protected pouch gives developing young a safer start than drifting exposed in open water.
