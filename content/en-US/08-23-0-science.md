A **lightning bolt** can reach temperatures of about **30,000 Kelvin** (roughly 30,000°C) — about **five times hotter** than the surface of the Sun (5,500°C). Despite this extreme heat, a lightning strike is incredibly brief, lasting only about 1-2 microseconds per stroke.
