Cleopatra VII lived in the first century BCE, but the Great Pyramid of Giza was completed roughly **2,500 years earlier**. That means Cleopatra is chronologically closer to the Moon landing than to the building of the oldest pyramid at Giza.

The comparison feels strange only because ancient Egypt often gets mentally compressed into one era.
