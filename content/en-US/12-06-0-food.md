True **wasabi** comes from the rhizome of *Eutrema japonicum*, a fussy plant that needs cold, clean water. Freshly grated, its heat is sharp and then gone in minutes. That makes the real thing expensive to grow and awkward to keep.

Outside Japan, and even in many Japanese restaurants, the green paste is usually **horseradish**, mustard, and food coloring. The sinus blast is related, but the flavor of authentic wasabi is greener, shorter, and less like a jar of relish.
