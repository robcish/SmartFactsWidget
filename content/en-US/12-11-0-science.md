At the University of Queensland, a funnel of pitch has been dripping since **1927**. The material looks like a black solid, yet it is a fluid so viscous that a single drop may take a decade to fall.

The experiment, begun by physicist Thomas Parnell, has produced only a handful of drops. It is a patient reminder that “solid” and “liquid” can be a matter of timescale, not just touch.
