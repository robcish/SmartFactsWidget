Bubble sort is famous today as the example of what **not** to use on large datasets, but it has deep historical roots. Variants of the method were already being analyzed in the 1950s, making it one of the oldest widely taught sorting ideas.

Its staying power comes from simplicity. Even though it is inefficient, the algorithm is easy to visualize, which is why it still survives in classrooms long after better practical sorts took over real software.
