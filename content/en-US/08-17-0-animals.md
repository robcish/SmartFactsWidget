A domestic cat's purr oscillates between **25 and 150 Hz**, a range that biomedical research has linked to increased bone density and accelerated healing of fractures, tendons, and wounds. This may explain why cats recover from surgeries and falls remarkably quickly compared to dogs.

Cats purr not only when content but also when injured or stressed, suggesting the vibration serves a **self-healing function**. Some researchers believe this is why cats have fewer bone and joint problems despite spending much of their lives sleeping.
