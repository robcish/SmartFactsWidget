In 2022, Jensen Huang argued that **Moore's Law was dead**, while Pat Gelsinger pushed back. The disagreement was not just about transistor counts, but about whether cost, speed, and practical computing gains were still improving on the old curve.

That is why announcements of its death keep returning. The law is part technical metric, part industry slogan, and part myth about progress.
