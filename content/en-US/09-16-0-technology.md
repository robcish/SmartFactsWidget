The Osborne Executive was marketed as a portable computer in 1982, but by modern standards it was **enormous and expensive**. Compared with an early iPhone, it weighed far more, took up vastly more space, and offered a tiny fraction of the processing power.

The comparison is memorable because it compresses decades of progress into a single object. Personal computing did not just get faster; it became radically smaller, cheaper, and more everyday.
