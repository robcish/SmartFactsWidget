Moore's original 1965 article predicted annual growth in transistor density, but he later **revised the timetable**. By 1975, he argued that doubling about every two years was a better fit for the industry's trajectory.

That update matters because "Moore's Law" was never a statute carved in stone. It was a moving forecast that engineers and companies kept testing against reality.
