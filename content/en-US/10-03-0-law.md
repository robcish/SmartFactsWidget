The phrase `You have the right to remain silent` comes from *Miranda v. Arizona*. The court held that suspects must be informed of key rights before custodial interrogation, so confessions are less likely to be coerced or uninformed.

A single legal decision ended up shaping police procedure and movie dialogue alike.
